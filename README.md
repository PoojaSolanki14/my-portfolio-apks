## How to install

1. Download the `.apk` on your Android phone.
2. Open the file. When asked, allow **Install unknown apps** for your browser or file manager.
3. Tap **Install**, then open the app.

## Good to know

- These are portfolio builds, not Play Store releases. Some are signed with a debug key, so Google Play Protect may show a warning.
- Each app has its own minimum Android version. The project documentation on the portfolio lists it.
- Full screenshots and a PDF write-up for each app are on the portfolio website.

- Links: every download link points to an asset that exists in your v1.0 release today. I checked the asset list earlier, and the earlier link check covered the ten new files. I didn't re-check the Balance Book and Talkzy links.
